WHO IS NIYAT?

Multidisciplinary by design.

Niyat Ghiorghis is a Business Information Systems graduate with a Sociology minor whose interests span technology, data, business, culture, design, visual storytelling, and writing.

Rather than separating technical and creative thinking, her work explores the ways both can inform how people understand systems, stories, information, and each other.
